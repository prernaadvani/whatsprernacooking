Butternut squash is the star of this show.

### Ingredients:

Per ~2 servings.

For squash:

* 1 butternut squash, cleaned and diced
* 1 shallot, diced
* 2 tablespoons EVOO
* handful of fresh parsley
* 1 teaspoon garlic powder
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* salt, to taste
* pepper, to taste

For salad:
* 2 cups cooked peal couscous
* 2 cups of greens
* Drizzle of EVOO

### Instructions:

* Preheat the oven to 375.

* Combine all the ingredients for the squash in a sheet pan. When the oven is heated, roast the squash for around 30 minutes, flipping halfway through.

* Once squash is cooked, assemble your bowl by adding some couscous, some greens, and some squash. Instead of making a dressing, I just drizzled some olive oil and salt on top which worked great. Enjoy!
